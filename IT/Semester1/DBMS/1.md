## Statement of Purpose - Amazon ML Summer School

I am currently pursuing a Bachelor of Technology in Information Technology at Shri Vishnu Engineering College for Women. My interest in Machine Learning developed through a growing curiosity about how intelligent systems process information, make decisions, and solve practical problems. As I strengthened my programming and problem-solving skills, I became increasingly interested in understanding how AI systems are designed and how data-driven models can be applied to meaningful realworld use cases. My journey into AI/ML began through self-learning, experimentation, and project-based exploration. While building my foundation in programming, I started learning core concepts such as supervised learning, neural networks, and model evaluation. However, what truly sustained my interest was applying these ideas to practical systems. Instead of limiting myself to theoretical concepts, I explored how modern AI systems process information, generate responses, and assist in decision-making. This curiosity gradually led me toward large language models and intelligent application design. One of the most meaningful experiences in my learning journey has been building an IPL chatbot project using Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG). The goal was to design an intelligent assistant capable of answering cricket-related queries using structured retrieval and contextual reasoning. During this project, I worked on data preprocessing, retrieval pipelines, prompt engineering, and conversational workflows to improve response quality and relevance. As the project evolved, I transitioned from a basic pipeline to a more modular multi-agent system, where different components handled tasks such as batting analysis, bowling insights, venue intelligence, and match-related recommendations. More importantly, this experience taught me how intelligent systems retrieve, organize, and synthesize information to generate meaningful outputs. It also made me curious about the deeper learning mechanisms behind model behavior, scalability, and performance. Alongside project work, participating in hackathons and technical events strengthened my ability to approach unfamiliar problems, learn quickly, and work effectively under constraints. These experiences improved my adaptability and reinforced my interest in building practical technology solutions. At the same time, I recognize important gaps in my understanding that I want to address. While I have explored AI applications and intelligent systems, I want to develop

a stronger foundation in the mathematical and algorithmic principles of Machine Learning, including optimization, deep learning, model generalization, and scalable ML systems. Building AI applications has made me realize that creating effective intelligent systems requires not only application-level understanding but also a strong grasp of the learning principles behind them. Amazon ML Summer School represents an ideal opportunity for me because it offers exposure to advanced ML concepts while enabling students to learn directly from scientists working on large-scale real-world systems. I am particularly excited to deepen my understanding of deep learning, generative AI, and recommendation systems while learning how ML models are designed and deployed at scale. My long-term goal is to build intelligent systems that solve meaningful real-world problems through AI-driven decision-making and automation. I believe Amazon ML Summer School will strengthen both my theoretical understanding and practical perspective, helping me contribute more effectively to future AI innovations.
